Patchwork Panic

The toy factory never closed. Repair every generator, then ride the elevator out before Grinwick catches you. Each floor is a new room, and the monsters get faster.

Move: WASD / arrows. Run: Shift. Fix: tap E or Space beside a machine, then tap again when the dial hand is in the green. Walking away cancels. Failed checks make noise. Turn the camera: drag with the mouse, or press Q and R. Phone: drag the left half to move, the right half to turn the camera, tap FIX. Black liquid slows you down. Side rooms hold torn notes: press E beside one to read it, and hide in there to break Grinwick's line of sight. From floor 4, a twisted quaker named Squawk perches in the rafters and dives at anything loud, so freeze and stay quiet when it screeches. Once all machines are fixed the elevator appears and you have 20 seconds to reach it, or you die. Beat floor 500 to escape the factory. Flutter training (unlock in the lobby for 120 seeds) lets you sprint 50% longer and run 40% quieter.
